02 / Background

From trading floors
to store floors.

I'm a product data scientist with an MS in Data Science from UT Austin. At Capital One I analyzed behavior across 3M+ customers to find what drove utilization and pricing sensitivity, and my analysis supported a Board-presented product feature reaching 1M+ monthly users. Before that I monitored trading risk at Goldman Sachs and built data pipelines as an engineer.

Today I run analytics and operations for my family's retail business, where I'm turning fully manual paperwork into data the business can actually use, and I help teach the graduate NLP course in UT Austin's MS Data Science program.

I work best where strategy and execution aren't separate jobs: defining the question, digging into the evidence, and shipping something people use.
