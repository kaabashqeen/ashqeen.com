01 Retired application · Case study & demo

YGOPLUS

A Yu-Gi-Oh! deck analysis product for competitive players: card search, deck construction, Monte Carlo opening-hand probabilities, and shareable combo boards. I owned the product end to end, from scope and probability design to UX, and used AI-assisted coding to build it.

It reached fewer than 100 users with no marketing, and no one adopted the paid tier. I retired it to cut hosting costs. The lesson: distribution was the bottleneck, not features. Next time I'd validate demand before building more.

Role
Founder, sole builder & product owner
Stack
Python, Flask, PostgreSQL, JS, Stripe, Cloudflare
Signal
Product ownership, Monte Carlo simulation, and cost-aware scope decisions
Audience
Competitive Yu-Gi-Oh! players and content creators

02 In progress · Operator systems

Retail data modernization

As managing partner of my family's retail business, I'm moving a fully manual operation onto data. Daily store paperwork is now digitized into a structured, analyzable record, and I'm migrating the item catalog and inventory into back-office software.

On top of that I track store-level KPIs (revenue, margin, cash flow, unit economics), analyze pricing and supplier spend across two stores, and built the financial analyses behind an eminent domain settlement and a business-closure settlement.

Role
Managing Partner, Operations & Analytics
Tools
Python, JavaScript, Google Sheets, back-office retail software
