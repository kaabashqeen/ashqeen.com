Try it yourself

Will your opening hand find a key card?

Choose a deck size and the number of cards that count as a match. Calculate the exact chance of drawing at least one match, then draw a sample hand.

Assumes a uniformly shuffled deck and drawing without replacement. “Matching cards” can represent one card or a group of interchangeable cards. This is a consistency calculation, not a win-rate estimate. Generic tiles illustrate the draw; the result does not depend on card effects.

How the probability is calculated

P(at least one match) = 1 − C(N − K, H) / C(N, H), where N is deck size, K is matching cards, and H is hand size. Each sample uses a fresh shuffle; one sample can succeed or fail regardless of the overall chance.
